# Sales Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales performance using Python, SQL, and Power BI.

The objective was to identify key revenue drivers, customer behavior patterns, and regional performance trends.

## Tools Used

* Python (Pandas, Matplotlib)
* SQL
* Power BI

## Key KPIs

* Total Revenue: ₹260M
* Total Profit: ₹42.9M
* Total Orders: 5000
* Total Customers: 996
* Average Order Value: ₹261K
* Profit Margin: 16.5%

## Dashboard Pages

### Executive Summary

Provides an overview of:

* Revenue
* Profit
* Orders
* Customers
* Monthly Revenue Trend
* Revenue by Region
* Revenue by Category

### Product & Customer Analysis

Provides insights into:

* Revenue by Product
* Profit by Product
* Top Customers
* Customer Revenue Distribution

### Executive Insights

Key Findings:

* Electronics contributes approximately 68% of total revenue.
* North region generates the highest sales.
* October records the highest monthly revenue.

Recommendations:

* Focus inventory on Electronics products.
* Increase marketing investment in high-performing regions.
* Investigate weaker revenue months.
